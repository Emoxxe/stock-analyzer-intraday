# Stock & Intraday Intelligence Terminal
> Built and engineered by Sahil. High-performance real-data Indian stock analytics and intraday momentum scanner.

## Primary Information
- **Domain**: https://stock-analyzer-intraday.vercel.app
- **Creator & Lead Engineer**: Sahil
- **Markets Covered**: National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) & Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE)
- **Primary Capabilities**:
  - Live Intraday Scanners: Opening Range Breakout (ORB), VWAP Pullbacks, RVOL spikes, and 9/20 EMA crossovers.
  - Candlestick Charting: Canvas-accelerated Lightweight Charts v5 with multi-timeframe analytics.
  - Market Hours Engine: Live tracking of Indian market trading sessions (Pre-open, Regular, Post-close).
  - Provenance & Data Integrity: Disclosed data sources and latency stamps with zero synthetic data.
